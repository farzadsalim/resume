Executive Summary

Platform and product executive building the governance infrastructure that lets global enterprises operate and trust what runs on their platforms at scale—identity and authorization, data security, and regulatory compliance—now extending that foundation to autonomous AI systems. Accountable for the data security posture, identity and compliance posture of Splunk’s $5B ARR platform, serving tens of thousands of enterprise customers including more than 90% of the S&P 500.

PhD research modeled and built authorization, delegation, and policy enforcement under uncertainty in distributed systems—the same primitives that determine how autonomous agents are delegated, constrained, and held accountable at runtime. That research has translated into a decade-long pattern at Salesforce and Splunk of turning access-control theory into shipped, enterprise-scale infrastructure: three products taken from concept to general availability.
